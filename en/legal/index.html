Privacy

What this site collects

Nothing. There is no contact form, no account to create, no newsletter and no shopping cart. There is no analytics, no advertising tracker and no social media pixel. The site sets no cookies and saves nothing in your browser.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub records standard server information when a page is requested, including the IP address that asked for it. Those records belong to GitHub. The shop does not see them and cannot get at them.

The map

The map does not load until you press the button to load it. When you do, your browser connects to Google, which receives your IP address and may set its own cookies. That connection is between you and Google, under Google's privacy policy. If you never press the button, this site contacts no other company at all.

Links to Google

The reviews and directions buttons open Google in a new tab. Once you are there, Google's terms and privacy policy apply instead of ours.

When you call or come by

That is a conversation, not a website visit, and this notice does not cover it. We write down what we need to do the work and to keep the records a repair shop has to keep: your name, a phone number, your vehicle, and what was done to it. We do not sell that, rent it, or hand it to anyone for marketing.

Children

This site is a shop's listing. It is not aimed at children, and it asks nobody of any age for personal information.

Terms of use

What this site is

A description of the shop and the work it does. It is not an offer, a quote or a contract, and nothing on it creates one.

Prices and estimates

No price here is a quote, because there are no prices here. The estimate for your car comes after somebody has looked at it. In New Jersey you have the right to a written estimate before the work starts and to approve the cost first. That happens at the shop, on paper, not on this page.

Hours and services

Hours, services and the makes we work on can change, and on any given day this page may be behind. Call before making the trip if it matters.

Warranty

Whatever warranty comes with a repair is the one written on your invoice. Nothing on this site adds to it or takes anything away from it.

Vehicle makes

The makes named on this site are named so you can tell whether we work on your car. They are the trademarks of their owners. JK Auto Repair is an independent shop, not an authorized dealer of any manufacturer, and not affiliated with one.

Photographs and text

The photographs are of this shop and belong to it. Please do not reuse them to stand for another business.

Accessibility

What we are aiming for

This site is built to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA. We test against them. We are a repair shop and not a software company, so we are not going to claim the result is perfect.

How it is built

Every page can be used with a keyboard alone and read with a screen reader. Every photograph has a written description. The text can be enlarged without anything breaking or overlapping. All of the information is in the page itself, so it still reads when JavaScript does not run. There is very little movement, and the site follows your system setting if you have asked for less of it.

If something is in your way

Call the shop. We will tell you anything this site says and book you in over the phone. You do not have to use the website to be a customer.
